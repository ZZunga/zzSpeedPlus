[Title]
zzSpeedPlus - Speed, Avg, Brevet, Lap, Max

[Description]
A speed data field with big, easy-to-read digits. Shows your current speed together with a second value of your choice, and colors your speed when you are faster or slower than average.
Built with the TitilliumWeb font for better readability on the move.
A rebuilt version of zzSpeed.

[Second value]
- Moving average (stops left out)
- Absolute average (stops included, brevet pace)
- Max speed
- Lap average
- None
- Units follow your device setting (km/h or mph).

[Colors]
- Current speed changes color when it is a set percentage (threshold) faster or slower than the average.
- Pick separate colors for faster and slower. Each color has a night and day version.
- The background follows your device setting (white or black).

[Two decimals]
- Turn on "Show two decimals" to see speed like 25.37. In one slot of a 10-field layout the unit is hidden to make room.

[Layouts, picked automatically]
- One slot of the 10-field layout: speedometer icon and second value on top, large current speed below.
- Wide slots: zzSpeedPlus title with the active mode (AVG / BRV / LAP / MAX) on top, both values side by side at the same size below.
- Same typeface, sizes and layout as the other Plus data fields, so they look consistent side by side.

[Supported devices]
Edge 540 / 550 / 840 / 850 / 1040 / 1050 / 1060

Change settings in the Garmin Connect IQ app or Garmin Express.
